copium.fun

Built around TxOdds: call the moment. Build the team. Keep the receipt.

Project description

copium.fun is a TxOdds-powered social sports game. When live odds or match context shifts, Officer Copium turns the moment into a 90-second YES/NO Pulse. Fans pick a side, compare themselves with the Crowd and TxOdds Line, and receive a shareable on-chain receipt when the moment resolves.

Fans can also build fantasy teams with captains and vice-captains, compete on live leaderboards, and create private rooms where friends play across Pulses and fantasy performance. TxOdds provides the live odds context; Solana devnet records the Pulse lifecycle, settlement, and proof.

Three-minute demo script

0:00–0:20 — Hook. “Every football match creates dozens of arguments: will they score, who is making the difference, and who actually saw it coming? copium.fun gives those arguments a clock, a leaderboard, and a receipt.” Show the live match feed and a Pulse countdown.

0:20–1:20 — Pulse. Open a Pulse from the web feed, show the Crowd vs Line split, make a YES or NO pick, and sign the Solana devnet transaction. Show the same Pulse delivered into Telegram or Discord if available.

1:20–2:05 — Fantasy and rooms. Create a private room, share the invite, then build an 11-player fantasy team within the credit and club limits. Select a captain and vice-captain, and show the room leaderboard combining match activity and fantasy performance.

2:05–2:35 — Resolution and proof. Let the Pulse close, open the receipt, show the result label, then follow the proof link to the settlement record and Solana transaction.

2:35–3:00 — Technical close. “TxOdds is the signal layer: it gives every Pulse a live Line and an odds snapshot to anchor the result. An Anchor program handles the on-chain lifecycle and settlement, so the experience is simple for fans but inspectable for everyone.” End with the demo URL and repository.
